Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026. This policy explains what information Katy Roofing Company TX collects through this website and what we do with it.

Information We Collect

When you fill out a form on this site, you choose to send us your name, phone number, email address, property details, and whatever you write in the message field. Our forms work through your own email application: when you press send, the message goes from your device to our inbox at info@katyroofingcompanytx.com. We don't operate accounts, databases, or registration on this website, so there is nothing stored on our end beyond the email you send.

How We Use Your Information

We use what you send to reply to your request: answering a question, scheduling an inspection, preparing an estimate, or following up on a project. If you ask us to stop contacting you, we do. We don't sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone.

Phone Calls

When you call us, we may note the details of your request the same way any service business does — so the person who handles your roof has the context of your first conversation.

Cookies and Analytics

This website sets no cookies of its own and runs no advertising trackers. If we later add analytics, this page will be updated to say so, in plain language, before anything starts collecting data.

Third-Party Content

Some pages embed content hosted elsewhere, such as the Google Maps view of our service area and photographs served from image hosts. Those companies receive standard technical requests (your IP address and browser details) when your browser loads their content. Their handling of that data is governed by their own policies, which we don't control.

External Links

Links to our social media profiles and other outside sites are provided for convenience. Once you leave katyroofingtx.com, this policy no longer applies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date. Material changes — new categories of data or new uses — will be described clearly, not buried in edits.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about information you've sent us: info@katyroofingcompanytx.com or (832) 662-6342. To see who we are and how we work, start on the homepage or the about page.
