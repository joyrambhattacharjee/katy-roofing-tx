Why We Work This Way

Katy gets the full menu of Texas roof punishment: hail that arrives sideways, straight-line winds out of spring storms, a sun that bakes shingle granules off ridge lines by August, and the occasional hurricane season that turns a missing tab into a soaked attic in one night. Anyone can hand you a bid after that kind of weather. The hard part — and the part we actually enjoy — is figuring out what the roof in front of you needs, which is rarely the same answer as the roof next door.

So our process starts with evidence, not a sales pitch. We walk the roof, photograph what we find, and show you. If a $400 flashing repair buys your roof five more years, that's the answer you get. If the shingles are done and a repair would just be money into a roof that's leaving, we'll say that too — with the photos that explain why. The fastest way to lose a customer in a community like Katy is to sell them the wrong roof, and word travels faster than any of our trucks.
