Roofing Questions, Straight Answers

How much does a new roof cost?

We don't publish price lists, because honest pricing follows the roof, not a menu. What a Katy roof costs depends on its size and pitch, the material you choose, how many layers come off, how much decking needs replacing, and how easily crews can work around it. We quote each roof individually after a documented inspection — in writing, with the scope spelled out so you can compare bids line by line.

Should I repair my roof or replace it?

It depends on the roof's age and what's actually failing. Isolated problems — one pipe boot, a stretch of flashing, wind-lifted ridge caps on an otherwise sound roof — are usually repair candidates. When shingles are past their service life, granules are washing into the gutters, or leaks keep showing up in new spots after repairs, replacement is usually the better spend. We photograph the roof so you can see the reasoning, not just take our word for it. The roof repair page has the full repair-versus-replace breakdown.

How do I know when my roof needs an inspection?

Schedule one after any hailstorm or wind event that left limbs down in your neighborhood, when you notice ceiling stains or granules in the gutters, before buying or selling a home, and roughly every two to three years once a roof passes age ten. You don't need to climb anything yourself — most early warning signs are visible from the attic or the ground.

My area just had hail. What should I do?

Check the soft metals first: gutters, downspouts, AC condenser fins, window screens. Dents there mean the roof likely took hits too. Don't let door-knockers climb your roof that day — ask for a documented inspection with photos of what was actually found. Hail bruising isn't always visible from the ground, but it shortens shingle life, so an inspection after a confirmed hailstorm is worth the hour. We cover this in more detail in our hail damage inspection guide.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most Katy-area homes are a one-to-two-day tear-off and install once materials are staged. Steep pitches, complex rooflines with valleys and dormers, tile, or metal run longer. Add scheduling around Gulf-coast rain systems, which can pause work mid-project — we build that into the plan and keep the roof watertight overnight, every night, until the last piece is on.

What roofing materials work best in Texas heat?

In greater Katy, the winners handle sun, humidity, and hail in combination: architectural shingles rated for wind and impact, standing-seam or exposed-fastener metal, and concrete or clay tile. The material is only half the system, though — ventilation is what keeps attic heat from cooking shingles from below. A cool roof with poor attic airflow still fails early, which is why we assess ventilation every time.

Do you handle commercial roofing?

Yes — flat and low-slope systems including TPO, PVC, EPDM, modified bitumen, and restorative coatings for buildings in the Energy Corridor and across greater Katy. Commercial roofs fail at seams, penetrations, drains, and curbs long before the field wears out, so our commercial work leans on targeted repair and maintenance programs to extend the roof you already have.
