Roofing in Richmond: What We See

Richmond gives us three different conversations depending on where the house sits. Around the historic core and older 77469 neighborhoods, we deal with genuinely aged roofs where the repair-versus-replacement question is honest work — sometimes a section of decking and a targeted repair preserves a home that deserves it, sometimes the roof has simply finished. In the established subdivisions of 77406 — Greatwood-adjacent country, Long Meadow Farms territory — the story is familiar: 1990s-2000s builder shingles at the end of their life, and hail events that accelerate the timeline.

The newest chapter is the growth corridor the Grand Parkway opened: master-planned communities pushing west and south, where first-roof problems are storm damage and builder-grade ventilation shortcuts rather than age. Big trees over young roofs is a Richmond specialty too — shade keeps shingles cool, then a storm drops a limb through them.
