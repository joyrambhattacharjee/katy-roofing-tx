Roofing in Brookshire: What We See

West of Katy, Brookshire's residential work is country roofing: homes on acreage, manufactured housing, barns, and the barndominiums that keep multiplying along 90 and 359. Metal is the natural material conversation here — panel roofs on shops and barns, standing seam on the nicer custom homes — and repairs skew toward wind damage and age, with wide-open exposure meaning storms hit these roofs with nothing in the way.

The other half of the map is the logistics boom: the distribution centers and warehouse space that followed I-10 west out of Houston. Those buildings run big, low-slope roofs — TPO and coated systems where the enemies are ponding, rooftop equipment traffic, seam fatigue, and expansion joints. Our commercial crews work that equipment list: drains, curbs, penetrations, unit flashing. For building owners and facility managers, we do assessments and maintenance schedules the same way we do for Katy's warehouse belt.
