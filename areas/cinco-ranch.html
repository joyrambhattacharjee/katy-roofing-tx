Roofing in Cinco Ranch: What We See

The neighborhoods built in Cinco Ranch's big growth years are arriving at the same place at the same time: original three-tab and early architectural shingles, now 20 to 25 years old, that have survived several hail seasons and are showing granule loss, curling, and recurring leaks around the same aging pipe boots. We handle a lot of phased work in Cinco Ranch — an inspection this year, a plan, and a replacement when the roof or the budget actually calls for it.

The tree canopy is the other defining factor. Mature oaks mean shade-side shingles last longer, algae streaks darken north slopes, and every serious wind event brings limbs down through roofs. And because Cinco Ranch's neighborhoods run on architectural guidelines, material and color selection has an extra audience — we plan estimates so what gets submitted to your HOA matches what actually gets installed.
