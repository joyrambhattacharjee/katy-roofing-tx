Accessibility

Last updated October 2026. We want every visitor to be able to reach us — including through this website.

What We've Built For

This site is a straightforward, no-framework HTML website, and that simplicity is an accessibility feature: it works without JavaScript, loads fast on slow connections, and behaves predictably. Beyond that baseline, we've built in:

Keyboard navigation throughout, with a skip-to-content link, visible focus states, and menus that open and close with the keyboard and close on Escape. Screen reader support via semantic HTML landmarks, a labeled navigation region, form fields with real labels (not placeholders acting as labels), error messages tied to their fields, and alternative text on every meaningful image. Color choices checked for contrast, and text that reflows at phone widths without horizontal scrolling. Motion kept to hover and focus transitions, and disabled for visitors who prefer reduced motion in their system settings.

Known Limitations

The embedded map of our service area is provided by Google and its accessibility is in their hands — the same information (the communities and zips we serve) is available as text on the homepage and areas pages, so nothing on this site is locked behind the map. Some form interactions use JavaScript as an enhancement for sending requests via your email application; without JavaScript, the same forms still submit through your email program.

Phone and Email First

For anything on this site that fights you — a form that won't cooperate, text that your assistive technology trips over, contrast that doesn't work for your eyes — skip the website entirely. Call (832) 000-0000 or email info@katyroofingcompanytx.com and a person will handle your request directly. And then tell us what went wrong, so we can fix it for the next visitor.

Tell Us

Accessibility problems are bugs, and reports are welcome at info@katyroofingcompanytx.com. Describe the page, what you expected, and what happened; we'll prioritize the fix. Our other site policies live on the privacy and terms pages.
