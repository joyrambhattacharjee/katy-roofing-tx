Storm Damage Roof Repair in Katy

Hail and straight-line winds off the Gulf are a fact of life here. A Katy hailstorm can bruise shingles that look fine from the driveway, lift flashing along ridges and valleys, and send tree limbs through a roof deck — and the leak often shows up weeks later.

After a storm, we check the whole system: shingles, exposed materials, flashing, vents, gutters, and edge metal. For damage you want to discuss with your insurance company, we document what we find with photos and a written report so your conversation with the claims process is easier. Coverage always depends on your policy and your insurer's determination — but understanding your roof's condition shouldn't be the hard part.

Roofing Built for the Gulf Coast Climate

Three things age a Katy roof faster than almost anywhere else in the country: heat, ultraviolet exposure, and hail.

Summer temperatures regularly push roof surfaces past 150°F. That heat bakes asphalt, weakens sealant at every flashing and penetration, and drives attic temperatures up unless ventilation is doing its job. We select materials and colors for that reality, size ridge and soffit vents so air actually moves, and detail underlayment and flashing for the thermal cycling a Katy roof lives through.

Then there's the weather side. When hail and heavy rain do arrive, the difference between a repair and a replacement is usually how well the system was installed in the first place — which is why we don't shortcut the parts you'll never see from the street.
