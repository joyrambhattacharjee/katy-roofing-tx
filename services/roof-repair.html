What We Repair Most Around Katy

Leaks around pipe boots and vents. The rubber gaskets on plumbing penetrations are usually the first thing Gulf heat destroys — they crack in seven or eight years, and every heavy rain finds the gap. Flashing failures. Valleys, chimneys, skylights, and wall transitions are where workmanship shows first; a badly lapped flashing leaks for years before anyone sees it.

Wind-lifted and missing shingles. Katy storms peel starter courses and exposed rakes even when the rest of the roof holds. Storm and hail damage. Bruised shingles, dinged vent caps, gutter and edge-metal damage. Localized deterioration. One slope baking in afternoon sun while the others stay shaded ages unevenly.

Whatever the symptom, the process is the same: a full inspection of the system, a clear explanation of what failed and why, and a repair matched to how much useful life the rest of the roof still has.

Repair or Replace? The Inspection Decides

Most roof problems are repair problems — but not all, and the difference matters when you're spending money. We recommend repair when the damage is localized, the surrounding shingles are sound, matching material is available, and the roof has meaningful service life left. We recommend replacement when granule loss is widespread, leaks recur in new places, prior repairs have stacked up, or the cost of repeated fixes starts closing the gap on a new system.

You get that assessment in plain terms, with photos of what we saw, before you decide anything. And if the honest answer is "this repair buys you two seasons," we say exactly that — sometimes the right call is the repair anyway, and that's your call to make, with real information.

Emergency Roof Repairs

When a storm puts a limb through the roof or a ceiling starts bulging, the first hours matter. We triage urgent calls first: tarping to stop water entry, securing exposed areas, and a documented look at what happened so you can act quickly with your insurer.

After the emergency is contained, the permanent repair gets scheduled with the same inspection-and-estimate process as everything else. If your situation is urgent, call rather than using the form — phone gets answered faster than email gets read.
