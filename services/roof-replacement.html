When a Roof Has Reached Replacement

Most of the Katy homes we replace roofs on fall into one of three groups: original builder shingles from the 1990s and 2000s booms now 20-plus years old, roofs that have taken several hail hits and have more granule loss than remaining life, or roofs whose repeated leaks tell us the failures are systemic rather than localized.

Replacement is a bigger decision than repair, so we treat it like one. The inspection covers the things that decide the answer: shingle condition across every slope, the age and remaining service life, storm history, the state of the decking visible from the attic, and structural condition around chimneys and valleys.

If a repair buys you five honest years, we'll say so. If it buys you one, we'll say that too.

The Replacement Process

  1. Inspection & Material Selection

    We assess the roof, then walk you through material options — architectural shingles, metal, or tile — matched to budget, home design, and Gulf weather.

  2. Written Estimate

    Scope, materials, timeline, and what the crew will and won't touch — in writing before anything is scheduled.

  3. Tear-Off & Deck Repair

    Old material comes off to bare decking; soft or damaged sections get repaired so the new roof starts on solid wood.

  4. Install & Final Inspection

    Underlayment, ice-and-water and synthetic barriers, flashing, ventilation, then the roofing itself — finished with a walkthrough and full cleanup.

Replacement Materials We Install

Material choice drives everything else: service life, wind and hail performance, energy behavior, and how the roof sits on your street. Architectural asphalt shingles remain the workhorse — proven in Texas heat, available in wind-rated and impact-resistant grades, and the most economical full replacement. Metal adds decades of service life and stands up to hail and wind particularly well. Clay and concrete tile suits the Mediterranean and custom homes scattered across Cinco Ranch and Seven Meadows, with the weight and framing checks that come with it. Whichever direction you lean, the system underneath — underlayment, flashing, ventilation — is where a replacement earns its longevity, and that's where we don't cut corners.

Roof Replacement Questions

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most Katy-area homes are a one to two day tear-off and install, weather permitting. Larger roofs, tile systems, and homes needing significant deck repair run longer — we give you the realistic number with the estimate, not the optimistic one.

Can you replace just one slope?

Sometimes, after a single-slope storm loss. It depends on age matching and manufacturer requirements for blending. The inspection answers this per roof — if partial replacement creates a mismatch or warranty problem, you'll hear it from us before the crew shows up.

What about ventilation during replacement?

Every replacement includes a ventilation check. If your soffit and ridge venting was an afterthought on the original build — common on builder-grade Katy homes — we correct it as part of the system so the new shingles age evenly instead of cooking from below. For why that matters so much in Texas heat, read our ventilation guide.
